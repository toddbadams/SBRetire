What the savings are worth today

The household’s accounts valued from the latest exports, cash included, in pounds. The total is the retirement model’s starting balance.

About these numbers

  • Analysis, not advice. Model calculations under stated assumptions; nothing here says what to buy or sell.
  • Past prices. Returns, risk and the frontier come from past monthly prices, a noisy guide to the future.
  • No tax. No capital gains, dividend or income tax is considered, and no tax wrapper rules beyond noting them.
  • Volatility is the annualised standard deviation of monthly returns.
  • Sharpe ratio is (annual return − risk-free rate) ÷ volatility: return per unit of risk.
  • Wrappers. Money in a pension or SIPP cannot move to an ISA or taxable account, so household-wide moves cannot be made as shown.
